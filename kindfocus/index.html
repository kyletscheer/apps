How it works

Say it like a sentence

Every focus and rule reads like something you'd say out loud. Tap a blank, choose what fits, read it back. No settings maze.

Set aside Social Media, except Maps, for 45 min, with one pause after a wait, and hold me to it.

Focus for now

Set a list aside right now, for a while. Save it as a setup and start it from your Home Screen.

Rules for every day

On a schedule, a daily allowance, or a few short visits a day. They run on their own.

Lists at the heart

Group the apps that pull at you. Add exceptions for the ones you always need.

Gentle, or steadfast

KindFocus meets you where you are, and lets you choose how firm to be.

A breath before every pause

Need something? Ask for a pause. First take a breath with a short wait that grows a little each time. Often that's enough to change your mind.

Or write an intention

Before a pause opens, say in a few words what it's for. "Check the bus times" is a reason. "Just bored" is an answer too.

Steadfast when you mean it

Make a focus steadfast and it can't be ended early. It finishes on its own when the time is up.

Rules that run on their own

Weekdays from 9 to 5. Thirty minutes a day. Twice a day for ten minutes. Set it once and let it be.

Start in one tap

Save your favorite focuses as setups and start them from your Home Screen or Lock Screen. A live ring shows what's left.

A quiet look back

Your week as a reflection, not a scoreboard: time in focus, the moments you chose calm, and what your pauses were for. No streaks. No guilt.
